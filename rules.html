THE CONSTITUTION

The governing document of The Commonwealth.

OFFICIAL FOUNDING DOCUMENT
PREAMBLE

WE THE PEOPLE

We the People of The Commonwealth, in order to form a more perfect union, establish justice, insure domestic tranquility, provide for the common defense, promote the general welfare, and secure the blessings of liberty to ourselves and our posterity, do hereby establish this Constitution and its laws.

ARTICLE I

Community & Conduct

  1. Treat all players with basic respect.
  2. No harassment, bullying, or targeted abuse.
  3. No hate speech or discriminatory behavior.
  4. No excessive chat spam.
  5. No impersonating staff or other players.
ARTICLE II

Survival & Gameplay

  1. PvP is allowed anywhere in the world.
  2. Stealing is permitted only during declared wars.
  3. Griefing is permitted only during declared wars.
  4. No hacked clients, cheats, or unfair advantages.
  5. No exploiting bugs for personal gain.
  6. No intentionally causing excessive server lag.
ARTICLE III

Land & Territory

  1. Players may establish their own bases and territories.
  2. Land claims are informal, not automatically protected by a plugin.
  3. Respect established bases and property.
  4. Do not deliberately build in ways that disrupt another player's base.
  5. Community builds must not be destroyed without permission.
ARTICLE IV

Nations & Warfare

  1. Players may establish nations and governments.
  2. Nations may form alliances and rivalries.
  3. Nations may declare wars against other nations.
  4. Stealing and griefing are allowed during declared wars.
  5. War declarations must clearly identify the nations involved.
  6. Players must not use wars as an excuse for harassment outside the game.
  7. No impersonating another nation or falsely claiming authority.
ARTICLE V

Server Security

  1. No attempts to crash or disrupt the server.
  2. No sharing private information about other players.
  3. No abusing staff permissions or commands.
  4. Report serious exploits and security problems to staff.
ARTICLE VI

Staff & Enforcement

  1. Staff may investigate reported violations.
  2. Punishments depend on severity and repeated behavior.
  3. Possible punishments include warnings, temporary bans, and permanent bans.
  4. Players may appeal punishments to staff.
  5. Staff must follow the same community rules.

ESTABLISHED BY THE PEOPLE
